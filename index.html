Our story

A Home Kitchen in Mount Waverley

The Punjabi Home Kitchen began the way most good food does — with neighbours asking for seconds. What started as cooking extra tiffins for nearby families grew into a little kitchen serving Mount Waverley with the food we grew up on.

Every dal is tempered by hand, every roti rolled fresh, every tray of Manchurian tossed to order. It's the food of a Punjabi home — hearty North Indian classics and the Indo-Chinese dishes every family secretly loves — made for the families around us.

If you're missing ghar-ka-khana, or you've just never had it, come taste the difference a home kitchen makes.
